# Reasoning

Answers can carry more than bindings. These pages cover matching that
is a judgment rather than a yes or no: matchers you write yourself, and
the measure algebra that scores, orders, and samples what they return.

- [Matchers and measures](./matchers-measure.md) writes custom matchers and guards the measure operations they feed.
- [Weighted relations](./weighted-relations.md) turns weight-producing Python callables into dual-mode relations for the measure operations.

Soft unification, proof search that scores structural closeness, grew
into its own library and lives in the sibling pettaprove repository.
